**Community set-level verdict:** "Commander-first set with thin but real
eternal impact" — one scary card (Fantasticar, mainly Vintage), one archetype
injection (Namor/Attuma for Merfolk), a handful of speculative engines.
Reviewers explicitly tempered Legacy expectations by analogy to the 2025
Spider-Man set.
